MARKET INTELLIGENCE TERMINAL v7 — OPPORTUNITY FUNNEL

WHAT IS NEW
- Keeps Fast Broad NSE architecture: broad discovery uses NSE bhavcopy, not 2,000 individual web calls.
- 3-stage opportunity funnel:
  Stage 1: liquid NSE universe
  Stage 2: delivery + volume + positive Nifty 500 RS
  Stage 3: RS acceleration + entry-quality filters
- Delivery Z-score: identifies unusually high delivery for that specific stock.
- Volume Z-score: identifies unusually high volume for that specific stock.
- Delivery and volume persistence over the last 10 sessions.
- RS acceleration: identifies improving relative strength, not only absolute RS level.
- Stock RS vs sector for stocks with a reliable industry mapping.
- Distance to 20-day high and price extension vs 20DMA.
- Volatility contraction proxy using close-to-close volatility.
- Extension penalty so a strong but already stretched stock is not ranked as a good entry.
- Opportunity labels:
  Early Accumulation
  Setup Ready
  Momentum Entry
  Watch
  Extended
  Distribution Risk
- Piotroski F-Score remains optional and is loaded only for the final shortlist to preserve speed.
- Entry Suitability Score now gives more weight to entry location, abnormal delivery/volume, RS acceleration and persistence.

HOW TO RUN ON WINDOWS
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the folder.
3. Double-click START_DASHBOARD.bat
4. The dashboard opens at http://localhost:8501

FAST WORKFLOW
Market regime -> broad NSE funnel -> accumulation/RS -> entry suitability -> optional Piotroski -> news confirmation.

IMPORTANT
The score prioritizes research candidates; it is not an automatic buy instruction. High delivery and volume can also occur during distribution, which is why price behavior, RS and extension are explicitly considered.
